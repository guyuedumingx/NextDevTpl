QuickStarter is our product foundation, extended from open-source software. Accounts, credits, a workspace, and administration provide a starting point for future products.

## Available today

Email registration, sign-in, credit history, settings, and administration work in the current local version.

## What comes next

Image and video generation are future directions. Model providers, background jobs, durable results, credit settlement, and live payments must be connected and verified before release.

This journal will document the changes we actually ship.
