# QuickStarter 品牌

名称：QuickStarter。品牌配置：`src/config/brand.ts`。页面共用：`src/components/brand.tsx`。

主色深绿 `#00594F`，辅助色浅玉绿 `#62B794`。Logo 为圆角 Q 形与向前的启程箭头。

使用内置 image_gen 生成，保留原图，透明 PNG 经尺寸优化后用于页面和 favicon。未使用外部现成品牌图标。

生成提示词：

> Logo-brand: create a single original minimalist geometric logo symbol for QuickStarter, a product development and creative software brand. A bold deep evergreen teal #00594F letter Q formed from a rounded square open frame, with a small diagonal forward-pointing cut at bottom right suggesting a launch arrow and fast start. One small jade #62B794 accent is allowed. Very clear, flat, vector-friendly shape with a strong recognizable silhouette at 24px. Transparent background. Centered mark only, filling 80 percent of a square canvas. No words, no wordmark, no tagline, no mockup, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no sparkles. Deliver exactly one symbol.

资源：`public/brand/quickstarter-mark.png`、`public/brand/quickstarter-icon.png`、`src/app/icon.png`。

不在页面中使用虚构用户评价或示例运营数字。不在未完成接入时声称图片、视频生成与正式收款已经可用。许可证与上游归属保留在仓库内。
